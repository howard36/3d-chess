# Verification: start

How to run this file: bring up the local server and client as in [the protocol](README.md#how-to-run-a-pass). Each section starts from fresh browser contexts (no stored seats) unless it says otherwise. Device values are defined in [devices and conditions](README.md#devices-and-conditions); `server stopped` means the local server process is not running (stop it with Ctrl+C and start it again when the step says so), and `storage off` means the browser refuses to store site data for `localhost`.

> **The pre-game flow was rebuilt at `1928567`** (and its wording and handover revised at `f38fcdb`, which the items below follow, as they do the still wait, the fixed seat spacing, the card docked beside the kings and the level liftoff since, and the plain link, the side buttons under the kings at every size, Random's button leaving with its coin and the guest's king lifted only at the arrival, and since then the card without its line, the side buttons sized with the kings, Random's king sliding into its seat, and every chosen king set down on the glass in its column of light, lifting only at the arrival, both kings together on both pages). "Start a game" no longer creates a game: it opens the side choice at `/new`, where the player picks White, Random, or Black among three kings on a sheet of glass, and the pick creates the game. The share-link, join, and joined screens are gone: the host sees an invitation to send ("Invite a friend") and the guest an invitation to the free seat ("You're invited to play …", "Join game"), both over the lobby's scene, and the game starts with an arrival and a handover to the board's entrance. The documents were rewritten from the code and not checked in the running app. The old items (CREATE-01 to CREATE-14, WAIT-01 to WAIT-12, JOIN-01 to JOIN-10) tested the old screens and are retired; their results are in this file's history before the rewrite. The items below are new, and none has been run: every Result reads `unverified`. (Main's own start-screen items, brought up there to the landing page without a pause or a line under its button, were retired with the rest at the merge; its claim about the title's and the button's bands is kept as CREATE-29.)
