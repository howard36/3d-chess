Each pair gives a ratio, head divided by base. A change is called **better** or **worse** only when every pair moves the same way, by more than 5% and by more than the pairs disagree with each other (the range of their ratios; from 10 pairs on, without the lowest and the highest). Otherwise the change is shown but not called.
