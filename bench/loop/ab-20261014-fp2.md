A change is called **better** or **worse** only when every pair agrees on its direction and it exceeds both 5% and the pairs’ own disagreement (the ratios’ range); otherwise it is shown but not called. A value at 0 on a side has no ratio: its change is the pairs’ difference, called when every pair moves the same way.
