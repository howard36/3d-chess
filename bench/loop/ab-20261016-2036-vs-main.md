A *pair* is one run of the base and one of the head, made back to back, so if the machine speeds up or slows down during the comparison, both sides feel it. Each pair gives a ratio, head divided by base. A change is called **better** or **worse** only when every pair moves the same way, by more than 5% and by more than the pairs disagree with each other (the range of their ratios). Otherwise the change is shown but not called. A value of 0 on one side has no ratio: that row is judged by the pairs’ differences instead, and called when every pair moves the same way. *Base first* and *Head first* are the geometric means of the ratios of the pairs where that side ran first: a gap between them that the overall figure lacks is the order of the runs, not the code (too few pairs to call anything on).
