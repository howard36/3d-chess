## Where the phone's board time goes (orchestrator item 1)

Traces (`BENCH_TRACE_COLD`, 5 loads a case) more than doubled the phone's board time under the 4× CPU slowdown (803–4018 ms), so the breakdown is from Resource Timing and the page's own stamps instead (4c5d5b0's `steps`), two untraced cold-load runs on fbac15f's app, 10 loads a case, medians in ms from navigation (`bench/out/steps/`, not committed). The traces still showed what runs where: the picture is painted into the display list as soon as it arrives (PaintImage at 491 ms in one trace) and decoded off the main thread (ImageDecodeTask 100–250 ms in the traces); the phone picture (2152×3172, `phone@2x.avif`, 38.6 KB on a DPR 3 phone) is drawn 1437.6 CSS px tall and ~975 wide in a 390×844 window, so about 24% of its pixels are on screen (SP3); Chrome counts no LCP for it (under 0.05 bits a pixel), so LCP cannot time it.

- **First visit**: ~175 ms for the page, then ~260 ms until the picture has arrived (one round trip plus ~150 KB through a 1 MB/s pipe: the picture competes with the entry and CSS for bandwidth; `vite preview` serves HTTP/1.1, where priorities don't share the pipe, while Pages serves HTTP/2 or 3, a bench-fidelity caveat), then ~200 ms decode and frame, overlapping the entry's evaluation (it arrives at ~537). Biggest parts: the picture's bytes and their place in the pipe, and its decode (4× more pixels than the window shows).
- **Returning**: ~170 ms for the page, ~170 ms revalidating the picture (a 304; its name has no hash, so the 0930:17 year rule does not cover it: SWR), ~85 ms before the page sees it (the main thread runs the cached entry from ~216), ~80 ms decode, ~75 ms to the frame. Biggest part: the revalidation round trip; next, the entry's evaluation in the way (the cache change's board cost).
- **Desktop** (board 217, 10 loads; no early picture there, SP5 parked): HTML 9, picture arrived 46, seen 108 (placed by React once the entry has run: entry arrived 49), decoded 183 (`wide.avif` 44.7 KB, 1x), painted 217. Biggest part: decode (~75 ms) and waiting for the entry to place the picture (~60 ms).
- **Measurement caveat (PB1, lens 28)**: the bench's stamp (load event → `decode()` → rAF) needs the main thread three times, while a decoded picture can reach the screen without it. When the entry runs, the stamp may lag the real paint. Check the stamp against screencast frames before trusting a few-percent board change; a stamp fix is the allowed measurement fix (own commit, both sides).
